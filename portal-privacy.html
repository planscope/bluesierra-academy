Privacy Policy

Blue Sierra Portal · Blue Sierra Construction · Effective November 6, 2026

The Blue Sierra Portal (bluesierracorp.com/portal) is a private project portal run by Blue Sierra Construction for its own clients, architects, field crew and team. This policy explains what information the portal uses and how we protect it.

Who can use the portal

Only people Blue Sierra Construction invites. There is no public sign-up. Each person only sees the projects they were added to, and only the parts their role allows.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a 6-digit PIN that is stored only as a salted hash.
  • Project content: messages, photos, files, plans, schedules, checklists, invoices and contract details that you or our team add to a project.
  • Activity: when you last opened a project or chat (for unread counts), and whether you are online (for "Who's around").

QuickBooks Online data

If Blue Sierra Construction connects its own QuickBooks Online company, the portal reads (and never changes) accounting records for our projects: customers, invoices and their PDFs, bills, expenses, timesheets and job profit and loss.

  • Invoices are shown to a project's client only when Blue Sierra chooses to share billing with them.
  • Costs, bills, payroll totals and profit are shown only to the company owner and team members he authorizes. Clients, architects and field crew never see them.
  • QuickBooks sign-in tokens are stored in Blue Sierra's private Google Apps Script settings, never in the browser and never shown to anyone.
  • Summaries are kept in a short-lived cache (about 15 minutes) so pages load quickly.
  • The connection can be turned off at any time from the portal (Home → Team → Disconnect) or from QuickBooks. The stored tokens are deleted when it is disconnected.

How we use information

Only to run our construction projects: to show each person their project, send notifications and login codes, and give our team an accurate picture of each job. We do not sell, rent or share your information for advertising, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to your project.

Where it is stored

In Blue Sierra Construction's Google Workspace account (Google Sheets, Google Drive and Google Apps Script), protected by Google's security. Email notifications are sent from our company Gmail. The portal's code is served from GitHub Pages and contains no personal or financial data.

Who we share it with

Nobody outside the project, except the service providers above (Google, and Intuit when QuickBooks is connected) that process data on our behalf, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

For as long as the project and its warranty period need it, or as required for our business and tax records. You can ask us to remove your access or delete your account at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by writing to info@bluesierracorp.com.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
